I. About

❧

I am a second-year mathematics PhD student at the University of Chicago. I am interested in algebraic geometry and its interactions with matroid theory and non-archimedean geometry. I am currently working on the computations of some KSBA moduli spaces.

Previously, I obtained my BA in Mathematics from Oxford in 2023 and completed Part III of the Mathematical Tripos at Cambridge in 2024.

\[ \mathcal{M}_{d} = \left\{ f : \mathbb{P}^1 \to \mathbb{P}^1 \;\middle|\; \deg f = d \right\} \big/ \operatorname{PGL}_2 \] — Moduli space of degree-\(d\) rational maps

II. Research

❧
My work is guided by questions at the boundary of algebraic geometry, combinatorics, and non-archimedean geometry.

Moduli spaces & matroid theory

I study the geometry of moduli spaces of curves and their connections to matroid theory. In particular, I am interested in the KSBA compactification of moduli spaces and the role of tropical geometry in understanding their boundary strata.

Non-archimedean geometry

I explore the interplay between Berkovich spaces and tropicalization, with applications to the study of degenerations of algebraic varieties and the skeleton of moduli spaces.
