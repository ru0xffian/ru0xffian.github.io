§ I. De Auctoris Vita et Studiis

❧

I am a fifth-year PhD student at the University of Chicago, advised by Prof. Miriam Harris. My research lies at the intersection of algebraic geometry and arithmetic dynamics. I am particularly interested in the geometric and arithmetic properties of moduli spaces of rational maps and their connections to dynamical systems over finite fields.

Before Chicago, I completed my B.S. in Mathematics at MIT (2021), where I worked on tropical geometry and combinatorial Hodge theory.

\[ \mathcal{M}_{d} = \left\{ f : \mathbb{P}^1 \to \mathbb{P}^1 \;\middle|\; \deg f = d \right\} \big/ \operatorname{PGL}_2 \] — Moduli space of degree-\(d\) rational maps

“The study of these moduli spaces reveals deep symmetries between algebraic geometry and dynamical systems.”

§ II. De Investigationibus

❧
My work is guided by questions at the boundary of algebraic geometry, number theory, and dynamical systems.
Algebraic Geometry

Moduli spaces of maps

Geometry of \(\mathcal{M}_d\), compactifications, singularities, and intersection theory.

Arithmetic Dynamics

Post-critically finite maps

Classification and arithmetic properties of PCF maps over number fields and finite fields.

Number Theory

Height functions

Canonical height functions and equidistribution theorems for preperiodic points.
