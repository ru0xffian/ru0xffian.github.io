About

I am a fifth-year PhD student at the University of Chicago, advised by Prof. Miriam Harris. My research lies at the intersection of algebraic geometry and arithmetic dynamics. I am particularly interested in the geometric and arithmetic properties of moduli spaces of rational maps and their connections to dynamical systems over finite fields.

Before Chicago, I completed my B.S. in Mathematics at MIT (2021), where I worked on tropical geometry and combinatorial Hodge theory.

\[ \mathcal{M}_{d} = \left\{ f : \mathbb{P}^1 \to \mathbb{P}^1 \;\middle|\; \deg f = d \right\} \big/ \operatorname{PGL}_2 \] The moduli space of degree-\(d\) rational maps — a central object in my research.

Research Interests

My work is guided by questions at the boundary of algebraic geometry, number theory, and dynamical systems.
Algebraic Geometry

Moduli spaces of maps

Geometry of the moduli space \(\mathcal{M}_d\) of degree-\(d\) rational maps, including compactifications, singularities, and intersection theory.

Arithmetic Dynamics

Post-critically finite maps

Classification and arithmetic properties of PCF maps over number fields and finite fields, with connections to the dynamical Mordell–Lang conjecture.

Number Theory

Height functions & arithmetic equidistribution

Using canonical height functions and equidistribution theorems to study preperiodic points and algebraic dynamics.
